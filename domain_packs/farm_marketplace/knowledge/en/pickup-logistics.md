## Who picks up my produce?
After you accept a bid, the app's transport partner schedules a pickup from your farm or
collection point.

## What is a return-trip pickup?
Sometimes a truck that has delivered goods nearby picks up your produce on its way back. This
return trip can make transport cheaper and faster.

## How will I know when the truck is coming?
The app shows the pickup date, a time window, and the driver's name. You can also ask the
assistant "when will my produce be picked up?"
