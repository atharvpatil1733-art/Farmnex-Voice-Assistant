## What is Crop Rescue?
Crop Rescue helps when your produce may spoil soon or a buyer backs out. The app alerts nearby
buyers right away with a quick-sale offer.

## When should I use Crop Rescue?
Use it when your produce will last only a few days, or when a confirmed sale was cancelled.

## Will I get a lower price in Crop Rescue?
Prices in Crop Rescue are usually lower than normal bids because buyers must act fast. You still
choose whether to accept an offer.
