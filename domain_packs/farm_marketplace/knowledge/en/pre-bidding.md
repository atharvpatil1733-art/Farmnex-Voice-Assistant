## What is pre-bidding?
Pre-bidding lets you list produce before harvest. Buyers place bids during the bidding window, so
you know the price before you harvest.

## How long does pre-bidding stay open?
Bidding stays open for 7 days from when the listing starts. You can accept a bid at any time during
those 7 days.

## What do I need to start pre-bidding?
The crop name, the quantity, and the expected harvest date. A photo of the standing crop is
optional but helps you get better bids.

## Do I have to accept the highest bid?
No. You can accept any bid, or accept none. If no bid is accepted before bidding ends, the listing
closes and you can list again.
